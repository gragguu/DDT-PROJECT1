Design Execution & Process Note

Slide 1: Grounding Our Interface in Core Design Principles

Our layout is structured to apply the principles of human-computer interaction, visual design, and behavioral models taught in the curriculum:

  • Typography System (Module 1.4): Pairing classical serif headers (Playfair Display) with an ultra-clean sans-serif body (Plus Jakarta Sans) establishes contrast, giving a premium and trustworthy feeling that suits a verified marketplace.
  • Design as Problem-Solving (Module 1.6 & 1.7): To address the lack of nutritional clarity, the layout emphasizes a clear hierarchy: identifying the consumer's problem, introducing our solution, and establishing immediate trust with verifiable data.
  • Hick's & Miller's Laws (Module 2.3): We chunked complex logistical processes and financial numbers into clear steps, three-column audience grids, and simple, interactive comparison charts to reduce cognitive overhead.
  • Aesthetic Consistency (Module 3): We selected a warm chocolate brown and muted gold color palette to match traditional Indian spices and snack colors, while maintaining high contrast to meet modern accessibility standards.

Slide 2: Interactive Features & Implementation Framework

To demonstrate a high-fidelity experience, the prototype features custom interactive elements designed around key UX guidelines:

  • Interactive QR Scanner Mockup (Module 6.1 & 6.2): Built using CSS Keyframes with custom timing loops. This interaction gives users immediate visual feedback and models a real-world scenario (anticipation, scanning, and clear results).
  • Smooth Motion & CSS Easing (Module 6.3): All micro-interactions use smooth transitions (`cubic-bezier(0.25, 1, 0.5, 1)`) to provide polished, professional feedback without overwhelming the user.
  • Data Presentation & Validation (Module 5): Instead of relying on static spreadsheets, we built interactive financial charts and vector-based layout models that adapt neatly to desktop screens, improving readability and data verification.
